# Acme Corp Travel Policy (fictional, for evaluation)

## Hotels
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to a nightly cap that depends on the city tier.
Tier 1 cities (Tokyo, London, New York, Zurich) have a cap of USD 300 per night.
Tier 2 cities (Lisbon, Kyoto, Prague, Bangkok) have a cap of USD 180 per night.
Stays above the cap need written approval from a cost-centre manager before booking.

## Flights
Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours.
Premium economy is allowed for flights between 6 and 10 hours.
Business class is allowed only for flights over 10 hours and only for directors and above.
First class is never reimbursed.
Flights must be booked at least 14 days before departure unless the trip is urgent.

## Rail
Rail is preferred over flying for journeys under 4 hours door to door.
In Japan the JR Pass is reimbursable when the itinerary includes three or more Shinkansen trips.

## Meals and incidentals
The daily meal allowance (per diem) is USD 75, or USD 95 in Tier 1 cities.
Alcohol is not reimbursable. Keep receipts for any single expense above USD 25.

## Insurance and safety
Corporate travel insurance covers all approved business trips automatically.
Travellers must register trips in the safety portal at least 48 hours before departure.
