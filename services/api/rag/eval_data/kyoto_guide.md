# Kyoto Guide (fictional notes, for evaluation)

## Neighbourhoods
Gion is the historic geisha district, best explored in the early evening along Hanamikoji Street.
Higashiyama has preserved wooden streets such as Ninenzaka and Sannenzaka leading up to Kiyomizu-dera.
Arashiyama in the west is known for its bamboo grove, which is quietest before 8 am.

## Sights
Fushimi Inari Taisha has thousands of vermilion torii gates; the full hike to the summit takes about two to three hours.
Kinkaku-ji, the Golden Pavilion, is busiest between 10 am and 2 pm.
The Philosopher's Path is a canal-side walk lined with cherry trees.

## Getting around
Buses cover most sights; an ICOCA card works on buses, subways and trains.
Kyoto Station connects to Osaka in about 15 minutes by Shinkansen and to Tokyo in about 2 hours 15 minutes.

## Seasons
Cherry blossom usually peaks in early April, and autumn leaves peak in mid to late November.
Summers are hot and humid, often above 35 degrees Celsius in August.

## Etiquette
Do not photograph geisha without permission, and avoid eating while walking in temple areas.
