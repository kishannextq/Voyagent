# Lisbon Guide (fictional notes, for evaluation)

## Neighbourhoods
Alfama is the oldest district, with steep lanes, fado houses and views from Miradouro de Santa Luzia.
Baixa is the flat downtown grid rebuilt after the 1755 earthquake.
Belem is home to the Jeronimos Monastery and the original custard tarts at Pasteis de Belem.

## Getting around
Historic tram 28 climbs through Alfama and Graca; board early to avoid queues.
The Lisboa Card includes unlimited public transport and free entry to many museums.
Lisbon is very hilly, so comfortable shoes matter more than in most European capitals.

## Day trips
Sintra, with its palaces and forested hills, is about 40 minutes by train from Rossio station.
Cascais offers beaches about 40 minutes west by coastal train from Cais do Sodre.

## Food
Try bacalhau dishes, grilled sardines in June during the Santo Antonio festival, and ginjinha cherry liqueur.
