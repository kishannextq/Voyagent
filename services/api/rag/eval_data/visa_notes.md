# Visa and Entry Notes (fictional, for evaluation; always check official sources)

## Schengen Area
Short stays in the Schengen Area follow the 90/180 rule: at most 90 days in any 180-day period.
Portugal is part of the Schengen Area.

## Japan
Many nationalities can visit Japan visa-free for up to 90 days for tourism or business meetings.
Japan requires a passport valid for the whole stay; some travellers also complete an online arrival form before landing.

## General
Carry proof of onward travel, since airlines may ask for it at check-in.
